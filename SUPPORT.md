# Plot Twist Support

Need help with Plot Twist? Start here — if your question isn't covered, email **sundaylogicapps@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you.

## Signing in

Plot Twist uses Sign in with Apple only — there's no separate username or password. If sign-in isn't working, check that you're signed into an Apple ID on your device (Settings → [your name]) and that you have a network connection.

## My shelf disappeared / isn't showing on my new phone

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used originally. Your shelf, ratings, and taste answers sync to your account, so signing in again on any device should bring everything back.

## A book's cover isn't showing / a "Get it" link looks wrong

Cover art and buy/library links come from third-party sources (Open Library and Bookshop.org). Occasionally a source has the wrong image or edition — email us the book title and we'll take a look.

## I want to delete my account

Open the app → **You** tab → **Edit Profile** → **Delete Account**. This permanently removes your account and everything synced to it, and can't be undone.

## Something else

Email **sundaylogicapps@gmail.com** with a description of what happened and, if possible, a screenshot — we read every message.

See also our [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md).
